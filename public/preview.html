Preview: a stand-in for a bwanabet.co.zm page
What you should see
  1. The Wheel of Fortune opens by itself (a player with an unused free spin).
  2. Spin it and see whether you won or lost.
  3. Close the wheel with its button, its red X, or a click outside the box: the Bonus Center is already open behind it.
  4. The daily reward pop-up follows (a Welcome back pop-up comes first for a logged-in player who earned rewards while away).
  5. Close the Bonus Center with the red X at the top right. ONE button (bottom right) does both: a gift box while the free spin is unused (opens the wheel), the gold coin from the moment you spin (opens the Bonus Center).

This preview uses the wheel's TEST mode: spins are simulated and never pay or record a real prize. The Bonus Center here is logged out (on bwanabet.com it uses the player's own login).
